# 90 Day Plan (1 Oct – 30 Dec 2026)

Targets: **10 components a month, 30 in total** (Track A) and **one agent log a week, 4+ a month, each a different kind of task** (Track B).

Weeks run Thursday to Wednesday, starting 1 October. Weeks with three components keep each month at 10. Week 13 is a buffer for polish and catch-up.
